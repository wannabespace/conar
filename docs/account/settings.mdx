## App preferences

Open your avatar menu → **Settings**. On macOS, you can also press **Cmd+,**.

| Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Light, dark, or system theme; transparent windows on macOS desktop. |
| **Keyboard** | Shortcut hints and a searchable list of shortcuts. |
| **General** | Usage analytics; desktop startup and database-link handling where available. |
| **MCP** | Desktop agent access, client setup, and activity. |

Preferences apply to this device. Turning off **Share usage analytics** stops usage events, error reports, and session recordings from it.

## Your account

Open [tamery.app/account](https://tamery.app/account) for your subscription, billing, [AI usage](/account/ai-usage), two-factor authentication, and signed-in sessions.

To sign out of the app, use the avatar menu. In the CLI, run `tamery logout`.

## Delete your account

On the account page, open **Settings → Account → Delete** and confirm. This permanently removes your Tamery account data; it does not delete the databases you connected to.

Cancel any active subscription before deleting your account.
