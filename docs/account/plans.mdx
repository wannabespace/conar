## Free

- Unlimited connections, synced across your devices.
- Table browsing and editing, SQL editor, and schema tools.
- One personal workspace.
- **20 AI filters** and **100 MCP queries** per week.
- AI help with connection errors and limited test-data generation.

Weekly limits reset on Monday at **00:00 UTC**.

## Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

- AI assistant, SQL editing, fixes, and completions.
- Unlimited AI filters, MCP queries, and test-data generation.
- Additional workspaces.
- Included AI credits.

Check [current pricing](https://tamery.app/#pricing) and start an upgrade from your [account](https://tamery.app/account).

## Early access

Both plans require an account with early access. Sign in to join the list; we email you when your account is ready.

## Billing

Manage your subscription at [tamery.app/account](https://tamery.app/account). **Manage billing** opens the billing portal for payment details, invoices, and cancellation.

After cancellation, Pro stays active until the end of your billing period.

For Enterprise pricing and deployment help, use **Contact us** on the [pricing page](https://tamery.app/#pricing).
