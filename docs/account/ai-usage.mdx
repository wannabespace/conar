Every AI request — chat, natural-language filters, Fix SQL, Update SQL — is recorded against your account. Open [tamery.app/account/ai-usage](https://tamery.app/account/ai-usage) to see it.

## What you see

Pick a date range with the picker above the table. It opens on the last twelve months, and the table covers whatever range you choose.

One row per month, newest first:

- **Requests** — number of AI calls
- **Input tokens** — tokens sent to the model, including cached ones
- **Output tokens** — tokens the model generated
- **Cost** — estimated price at the model's list rate, from [LiteLLM's public price sheet](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/blob/main/model_prices_and_context_window.json)

An answer you stop mid-generation is not counted. The model provider never reports token counts for an interrupted response, so there is nothing to record.

<Note>
  Cost is an estimate for reference. Your Pro subscription covers AI usage; you
  are not billed per token.
</Note>
