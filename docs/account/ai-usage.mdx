Open [AI usage](https://tamery.app/account/ai-usage) in your account. Choose a date range to see monthly totals.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Requests** | AI calls recorded for your account. |
| **Input tokens** | Text and context sent to the model, including cached input. |
| **Output tokens** | Text generated by the model. |
| **Cost** | Estimated model cost for those requests. |

**Cost** is a usage estimate, not an invoice or an extra per-token charge. For your subscription and payments, open **Billing** in your account.
