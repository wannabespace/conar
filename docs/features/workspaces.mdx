A workspace groups connections. Use one for personal work and another for a client or project.

You get a personal workspace when you sign in. Switch workspaces using the workspace name in the title bar; Tamery shows the connections in the selected workspace.

## Create a workspace

Requires [Pro](/account/plans) and an internet connection.

1. Open the workspace switcher.
2. Choose **Create workspace**.
3. Enter a name and click **Create**.

New connections go into your active workspace. Each device remembers its own active workspace, and you can switch between existing workspaces offline.

Workspaces are personal to your account. Teammate invitations, shared workspaces, and workspace deletion are not available.
